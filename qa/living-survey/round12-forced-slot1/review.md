Browser-only route fulfillment intercepts current Vite `living-render.ts` output, asserts three exact hook tokens, and changes only the in-memory response. All production files remain unchanged; external APIs aborted. The QA source is a clone of the existing rated34/range22 warm high lamp, positioned [191,3,57], aimed [199,0,64]. No intensity, range, angle or profile increase. Total key slots remains two.

The decorative fixture is not moved: this is explicitly a lighting-path diagnosis, not a physically assembled scene.

Thus this test replaces the floor-pool lamp at the fixed view; it does not preserve that pool, and its loss must not be hidden in the comparison. Actual live far-key intensity822.8 equals the existing rated34 lamp's live conversion, not an increased rating. Recording uses the normal separate profile; recorded slot outputs are available rather than inferred from profile names.

Observed: both live poses now show a small warm patch of far wall/panels and railing above center. Hence the lamp can reach at least that visible far surface; tanks do not completely occlude it. This establishes selection exclusion as a cause of the earlier missing direct illumination. It does not prove a continuous architectural depth line. The patch remains isolated, and fixed view sacrifices the useful ground-level pool. Film does not justify accepting the live regression.

Decision: retain diagnosis and reject this as a production visual solution. No changes integrated. A future L2-specific light-selection design could reserve the far source while preserving the ground source within the same two-key budget, then assess tank separation and transitions as the camera advances. That is a design hypothesis requiring another controlled visual check, not authorization to alter the shared renderer or a claim of AAA quality.
